Acme Corp — Financial Report: Q1 2024 (January – March 2024)

Executive Summary:
Q1 2024 was a strong quarter for Acme Corp, with total revenue reaching INR 12.4 Crore, representing a 23% year-over-year growth. Operating profit stood at INR 2.8 Crore (margin: 22.6%). Customer acquisition increased by 18% compared to Q1 2023.

Revenue Breakdown:
- SaaS Subscriptions: INR 7.2 Crore (58% of total revenue)
- Professional Services: INR 3.1 Crore (25% of total revenue)
- Licensing: INR 1.5 Crore (12% of total revenue)
- Other: INR 0.6 Crore (5% of total revenue)

Top Revenue-Generating Regions:
1. Mumbai: INR 3.8 Crore
2. Bengaluru: INR 3.2 Crore
3. Delhi NCR: INR 2.7 Crore
4. Pune: INR 1.4 Crore
5. Hyderabad: INR 1.3 Crore

Expenses:
- Employee Costs (salaries, benefits): INR 5.2 Crore
- Infrastructure & Cloud: INR 1.8 Crore
- Sales & Marketing: INR 1.6 Crore
- R&D Investment: INR 0.7 Crore
- General & Administrative: INR 0.3 Crore
Total Expenses: INR 9.6 Crore

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR): INR 4.1 Crore
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): INR 49.2 Crore
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): INR 18,500
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV): INR 2,40,000
- Churn Rate: 2.1%
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 118%
- Gross Margin: 68%

New Customers:
Total new enterprise customers in Q1: 47
Total new SMB customers in Q1: 312
Customer base at end of Q1: 1,842 active accounts

Product Performance:
- Copilot Pro Plan: 68% of new signups
- Copilot Business Plan: 24% of new signups
- Copilot Starter Plan: 8% of new signups

Outlook for Q2 2024:
- Revenue target: INR 14.5 Crore (17% QoQ growth)
- Planned hiring: 12 new engineers, 5 sales executives
- New product launch: AI Workflow Automation module (April)
- Target markets: Chennai, Ahmedabad, Kolkata

Risks & Mitigations:
- Risk: Market slowdown in IT spending. Mitigation: Focus on cost-saving ROI messaging.
- Risk: Increased competition. Mitigation: Accelerate product differentiation.
- Risk: Talent attrition. Mitigation: Revised compensation bands and ESOP grants.
