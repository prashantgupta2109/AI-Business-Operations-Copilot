Acme Corp — Human Resources Policy

Recruitment & Onboarding:
All new hires must complete a 30-day onboarding program.
Background verification is mandatory for all positions.
Probation period is 3 months for all new employees.
A buddy system pairs new employees with a senior team member for the first 60 days.

Performance Management:
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually — in April and October.
Each employee sets OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) at the start of each quarter.
Ratings are: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations.
Employees rated Below Expectations for 2 consecutive quarters enter a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

Benefits:
Health insurance covers the employee, spouse, and up to 2 children.
Group life insurance is provided at 3x annual salary.
Gratuity is applicable after 5 years of continuous service.
Internet reimbursement of INR 1,500/month is available for remote employees.
Meal allowance of INR 3,000/month is provided to all full-time employees.

Learning & Development:
Each employee receives an annual L&D budget of INR 25,000.
Online courses, workshops, and conferences are covered under the L&D budget.
All certifications funded by the company require a 1-year retention commitment.
Internal training sessions are conducted monthly by senior leaders.

Leave Types:
- Casual Leave: 12 days/year
- Sick Leave: 8 days/year
- Earned Leave: 20 days/year
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (as per law)
- Paternity Leave: 10 days
- Bereavement Leave: 5 days
- Marriage Leave: 3 days

Resignation & Exit:
Notice period is 60 days for all employees.
Full and final settlement is processed within 30 days of last working day.
Exit interviews are conducted by HR within the last week of employment.
All company assets must be returned before clearance is issued.

Grievance Policy:
Employees can raise grievances confidentially via the HR portal.
All grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 14 working days.
Retaliatory action against any employee who files a grievance is strictly prohibited.
