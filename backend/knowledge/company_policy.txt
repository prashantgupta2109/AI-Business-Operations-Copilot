Acme Corp — Company Policy

Work Hours:
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible start times are allowed between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM with manager approval.
Overtime beyond 9 hours/day must be pre-approved by the department head.

Work From Home (WFH):
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
WFH requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance via the HR portal.
Remote employees must be online and responsive during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM).

Leave Policy:
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 paid leave days per year.
Leaves reset on January 1st and cannot be carried forward beyond 5 days.
Emergency leave (up to 3 days) may be taken without prior notice but must be documented.

Payroll:
Salary is processed on the last working day of each month.
Salary revisions are reviewed annually in April.
Bonus payments are made in December based on performance ratings.

Code of Conduct:
All employees must maintain respectful and professional behavior at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying will not be tolerated and will result in immediate action.
Confidential company information must not be shared outside the organization.

IT & Equipment:
Company-issued laptops are for professional use only.
Personal data must not be stored on company devices.
All software installations require IT department approval.

Expense Reimbursement:
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence.
Expenses above INR 5,000 require a manager's sign-off.
Travel expenses must include original receipts and boarding passes.
